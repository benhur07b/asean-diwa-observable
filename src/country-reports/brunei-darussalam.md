## Key findings

<div>
Brunei Darussalam shows a strong foundation in digital access, with near-universal mobile phone ownership and national initiatives to improve broadband infrastructure. However, a significant gap exists between technological readiness and policy maturity in gender equity. The country has several ad-hoc initiatives promoting digital skills for women and girls but lacks a comprehensive legal or policy framework to address gender disparities in the ICT sector systematically. Women's participation in the ICT workforce and as graduates in ICT programs has been highly volatile, without a clear upward trend.
<br>
<br>
The available data for Brunei is sparse and inconsistent, which presents a major challenge for effective policymaking. Many key indicators, such as the gender pay gap and women in ICT management roles, are not reported at all. Other metrics have only a few data points, making it impossible to identify long-term trends. For ASEAN’s gender and ICT mainstreaming agenda, Brunei’s case underscores the critical need for systematic, disaggregated data collection. Without more robust and consistent data, efforts to design targeted interventions to ensure women's equitable participation in the digital economy will remain reactive rather than strategic.
</div>

## Categorical indicators

<div>

| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Gender Equality Policies**</span>| Brunei does not have one comprehensive law specifically addressing gender gap in their country. However, they have made some sector-specific efforts to address gender discrimination. A report made by the World Bank in 2020 summarizes these and reveals that the overall score for Brunei Darussalam is lower than the global average score. Brunei has also set to release their National Plan of Action on Women in 2023 as a part of their 2035 Vision but publications of this are not available on the internet. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Workplace Equality Policies**</span>| Based on World Bank's 2024 Women, Business, Law Report, the government has not yet published guidelines on non discrimination based on gender in recruitment in any job sector, including ICT. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Gender and ICT Policies**</span>| Brunei currently does not have policies integrating gender and ICT. What they have are action plans such as their Digital Economy Masterplan 2025 published in 2020. The action plan pushes for an inclusive digital economy yet it has no specific clauses on gender equality. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Education and Development Initiatives**</span>| The country relies on targeted programs rather than a national policy. Initiatives include the annual "Girls in ICT Day" and the "Digital for All" program, which provides general digital upskilling. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Entrepreneurship Programs**</span>| The "IDEAS Project" (2024) is a key initiative aimed at empowering women entrepreneurs across ASEAN, including in Brunei, by enhancing their digital skills for business. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Gender Budgeting**</span>| Section 2.2.4 of Brunei's National Report for 2020-2024 does not outline a specific budget plan to address gender disparity in the country. It only mentions previously-implemented projects to provide financial support to marginalized women and girls. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Specialized Government Bodies**</span>| No specialized government body dedicated to gender and ICT exists. Responsibilities are split between the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports for gender initiatives and the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry (AITI) for the ICT sector. |
| <span style="color: rgb(200, 5, 124);">**Data Collection and Analysis**</span>| While Brunei has conducted National ICT Surveys since 2013, they are usually focused on household data and just aggregated by gender when applicable. They still don't have ICT surveys which aim to address gender gaps in ICT.

## Numerical indicators
<div>
The quantitative data for Brunei Darussalam is fragmented, with many critical indicators entirely unreported, including women in ICT leadership, the gender pay gap, and enrolment and completion rates in ICT courses. The available data on workforce participation, educational attainment, and basic access is often inconsistent, with metrics recorded for non-consecutive years, which makes it difficult to establish reliable long-term trends. This scarcity of data limits a comprehensive assessment of the digital gender divide and highlights a foundational challenge for evidence-based policymaking.

Their share in Engineering, Manufacturing and Construction is variable, though it reached 52.3% in 2018. Overall, women are highly represented in traditionally female-dominated fields. |

The overall trend shows a decline, with the percentage falling from 63.0% in 1999 to 41.9% in 2018. |

## Gender Gap in Internet Access

<div class="card">
The difference between the percentage of women and the percentage of men who use the internet within a given population. 

A positive value indicates women generally have more access while a negative value indicates they generally have less.
</div>

```js
const indicators_gender_gap_internet_access = FileAttachment("../data/indicators_numerical_gender_gap_internet_access.csv").csv({typed: true});
```

```js
const indicators_gender_gap_internet_access_filtered = indicators_gender_gap_internet_access.filter(row => row.country === 'Brunei Darussalam')
  .map((row) => {
    return { 
      year: (new Date(parseInt(row.year), 0, 1)), 
      indicator_value: parseFloat(row.indicator_value)
    }
  })
```

```js
Plot.plot({
    x: {interval: Plot.timeInterval("1 year")},
    y: {label: "Gender Gap in Internet Access (%)"},
    marks: [
        Plot.barY(
            indicators_gender_gap_internet_access_filtered, 
            {x: "year", y: "indicator_value", r: 16, fill: "#C8057C",  tip: true}),
        Plot.axisX({label: "Year"}),
        Plot.axisY({label: "Gender Gap in Internet Access (%)"}),
    ]
})
```
